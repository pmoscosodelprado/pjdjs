WHO ARE WE

We're PJ.DJS. A DJ duo from Barcelona bringing sweaty dancefloors, guilty pleasures and big smiles wherever we play.

Genres are optional. Dancing isn't. Our sets jump between house, disco, vocal classics, indie dance, pop remixes and unexpected anthems.

Britney. Beyoncé. Madonna. Purple Disco Machine. Robyn. Romy. Everything is fair game if the dancefloor loses its mind.
